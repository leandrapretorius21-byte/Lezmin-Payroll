Choose the Excel file, for example SEP 2026 salaries Cleaners.xlsx. The names, days, leave, Sundays, overtime and bank details are read from it, and every payslip is worked out for you to check and print.

Staff this month

People added or changed here only appear on this month's payslips. To keep them for next month, add them to the Excel sheet too, or use Export to Excel and import that file next time.
